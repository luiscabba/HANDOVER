## Platform and portal

| Date | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 13 Oct | SBC refers companies to checked local agents rather than acting as their agent. Thin agent panel in prototype scope | Working answer for SBC to confirm |
| 15 Oct | Start with the officer side (review console), as canvas mockups before build | The review is the core of the business |
| 15 Oct | Company and buyer portals use the console look, not the website look, but keep delight moments. The website's night hero is used for the SBC-Qualified moment | Work tool vs marketing site |
| 15 Oct | Officer queue is a kanban board. Each column's goal clear at a glance. Only high-level info on cards; AI detail in pop-up sub-windows | Officers triage, then dig in |
| 15 Oct | Check screen: three panes (left checklist and status, middle AI finding with files and actions, right document viewer). Checks in fixed order. AI flag only where needed. Hand-off button is just "Send". "Request" sits on the file row. No "your job" or "You" tags, no explainer lines | Should feel like a document-approval pipeline with AI's part made concrete |
| 15 Oct | AI thinking is shown with the SBC cube animated, 2D and small. The AI then asks its findings as questions for approval. This is the standard for all officer screens | Human approves every AI finding |
| 15 Oct | Officer side feels like an admin account: demo login with autofill | Client demo realism |
| 16 Oct | **Rules first, AI later.** Build the core on business rules, workflows, matching logic and approvals; introduce AI in controlled stages, autonomy growing with confidence thresholds | Ian's counter to Khaled's "AI operates, humans govern" ecosystem; approved by SBC |
| 3 Nov | Four-stage AI ladder: rules only, AI suggests, AI acts by threshold, agent network | Matches Ian's plan. Map the rules-first architecture before anything else |

## Rejected, do not bring back

- Palette B1 "The Record" (seal red) and the beige/grey limestone look for the 3D model
- The Seal mark's double-line outline
- Hatched lattice finish
- Stone cube still-life photos on Home
- The word "checked" and the line "Checked before introduced"
- A Docs-based deliverable set: deliverables are canvases and working sites
- Realistic 3D cube for the AI thinking state (keep it 2D and small)
- Explanation lines and "You" tags on officer screens
- An AI-first platform where agents operate and humans only govern (superseded by rules first)
