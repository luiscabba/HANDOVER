# SBC Capital design

## Tokens (from `sbc-capital-site/assets/site.css`)

| Role | Day | Night |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#FFFFFF` | `#1A1D24` (ink) |
| Text | `#1A1D24` | `#FFFFFF` |
| Muted text | `#5B6070` | `#A9AFBA` |
| Lines | `#CFCBC3` (limestone) | `#2A2F3A` |
| Primary button | `#1A1D24` | `#1F4FD1` (cobalt) |

Type: Readex Pro (300, 400, 600) for everything; IBM Plex Mono 500 for reference numbers and data.

Marks: C3 solid lattice cell = SBC Capital. S3b indicator (SBC in an outline with a lit square) = SBC-Qualified. Never swap them.

Motion: parallax, 3D on scroll, image to image. The SBC-Qualified card turns in 3D and stays 3D on phones. AI thinking = the cube, 2D and small, animated.
