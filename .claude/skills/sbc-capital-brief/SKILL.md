# SBC Capital brief

1. Read, in order: `sbc-capital/README.md`, `sbc-capital/DECISIONS.md`, `sbc-capital/ROADMAP.md`, `sbc-capital/DESIGN.md`.
2. Check the code for drift: `git -C sbc-capital/code/sbc-capital-site log -5 --oneline` and the same for `sbc-portal-preview`. If commits are newer than the last dated decision, read them and note what changed.
3. Report back in under 15 lines: what is settled, what is open (from "Waiting on SBC"), and the next roadmap item worth doing.

## Rules that are easy to break

- Always "SBC Capital", never "SBC" alone, in anything client-facing.
- Two marks, two jobs: C3 lattice cell = the company; S3b lit-square indicator = SBC-Qualified.
- The review has five checks. The site is the source of truth.
- Rules first, AI later. Do not design features where AI acts without officer approval until stage 3 of the ladder is agreed.
- On officer screens, AI findings are asked as questions for approval; AI thinking is the small 2D animated cube.
- Portals use the console look; the website look is for the public site and the SBC-Qualified moment only.
- Never use the word "checked" in copy.
- Unknown facts go in as `[brackets]`; assumed ones are tagged as working answers.
