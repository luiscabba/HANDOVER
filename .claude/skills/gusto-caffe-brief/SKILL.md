## Rules that are easy to break

- The site has one job: turn a B2B buyer into a WhatsApp or email inquiry. No cart, payments or CRM.
- No WebGL. 3D objects are Blender renders shipped as scroll-scrubbed frame sequences.
- No flat, static text sections. Every section moves with the scroll.
- "Produced by Italian Hand" is gone everywhere, including bag labels.
- Do not bring back the seal, crest or old wordmark logos.
- Arabic is right-to-left with motion mirrored; texxen writes the Arabic.
- Aida Gasmi is the only approver. Anything she has not supplied stays `[bracketed]`.
- Sandwiches are the memorable moment of the next version; the bar shows drinks one by one.
