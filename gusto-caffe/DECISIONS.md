# Gusto Caffè decisions

Dates are 2026. The project pivoted on 27 Oct; anything before that is marked "pre-pivot".

## Business and scope

| Date | Decision |
|---|---|
| Sept | Market is Egypt. B2B only (cafés, hotels, shops buying in bulk). Local orders only |
| Sept | Lead generation only: inquiries go to WhatsApp or email. No CRM, cart or payments |
| Sept | Bilingual Arabic and English |
| 27 Oct | Pivot treated as a new project: logo redesign plus a scroll-animated site in the spirit of FERARA Cafe, with Gusto's own branding |
| 27 Oct | The "Produced by Italian Hand" tagline is dropped everywhere, including redrawn bag labels |
| 27 Oct | Engagement order: client confirms the worklist, then quote, then work starts. texxen handles Arabic translation |
| 28 Oct | Site is a B2B sales story: Hero, Clients, Coffee, (Coffee menu), Machines and training, Sandwiches, How to order, Our story, Locations, Contact |

## Logo

| Date | Decision |
|---|---|
| 27 Oct | Logo work starts from themes and motifs (Italian flag, family, 2008) that carry into the site |
| 27 Oct | Shortlist went to client: seal R2, crest oval, wordmark O2.2 (dark and light) |
| 4 Nov | **Client rejected the whole shortlist** as an older generation. They liked a cursive logo in their examples |
| 4 Nov | Cursive round: one direction should carry the feel of the client's reference (gold brush script, cup as the O, green and red steam, brown Caffè) without copying it |
| 4 Nov | Aida asked to also use Gusto's own cursive logo (gold brush GUST, cup O, Caffè) with the steam refined and outlined in black so the flag colours stand out. Added as variation 5 |
| 12 Nov | Presented cursive A to D plus 5a and 5b. Waiting on the pick |

## Site

| Date | Decision |
|---|---|
| Oct (pre-pivot) | Notte direction: dark espresso, cinematic. Kept after pivot as the tone |
| 27 Oct | Keep from pre-pivot: marble bar counter, "Read the bag" annotated pack, Italy-to-Egypt route map (adapted), Notte dark tone |
| 27 Oct | Scroll references: Apple AirPods-style sticky scenes, Jazean's material hero, motion mirrored for Arabic |
| 27 Oct | Flow must be seamless like a polished SaaS site. No flat static text sections |
| 28 Oct | Play with camera, POV and 3D space |
| 28 Oct | Until Gusto sends assets, use typical French and Italian café items as placeholders |
| 28 Oct | **No WebGL.** 3D items (bags, beans, cups on the marble bar, hero counter) are photoreal Blender renders shipped as Apple-style scroll-scrubbed frame sequences. Sandwiches and the machine stay photo cutouts |
| 28 Oct | Slower scroll pacing with a short trailing ease. Original Gusto bag images reused |
| 3 Nov | Next version: one continuous world, drinks shown one by one, realistic café items, sandwiches as the memorable moment. Tried Nate Herk's Scrollcraft skill (github.com/nateherkai/scroll-craft) and kie.ai for generated assets |

## Rejected, do not bring back

- Logos: seal, crest, Ceralacca, O1.2 heavy gold caps, the cup "O" and steam on the original wordmark (found tacky). The whole pre-cursive shortlist
- Bottega and Tricolore site directions (exist as `/a` and `/b` builds on the old directions site only)
- Glass chat "Where to buy" and the one-question-at-a-time contact form
- WebGL 3D in any form
- Static, flat story sections; the flat family-business story block
- Slogan headers. Use plain section names
